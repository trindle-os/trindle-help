Every word Trindle shows can be changed. You can say "Open it, brew" where Trindle says "Click here to open the file", or write a whole language Trindle does not have yet. Your words are kept in one file, a word sheet, and every Trindle program reads it, so one change shows everywhere.

Your own words are in Settings, Region and language, in the card called Your own words.

## Change a word

1. Open Settings, then Region and language.
2. In Your own words, choose the language the sheet is for.
3. Under Find a word, type part of what Trindle says now. The words that match are listed, each with a box beside Trindle's English.
4. Type your own words in the box. What you leave alone stays as it was.

## Share your words

Export the sheet to save it as a file you can give to anyone. Import a sheet to use one somebody gave you. You hand the file over yourself, as you would any file. Trindle never sends it anywhere.

An imported sheet is checked as a whole before any of it is used. It must be a plain text file Trindle made, with nothing added to it that is not words. If anything is wrong, nothing is installed and the card says what was wrong.

Questions: how do I share my words; how do I import a word sheet; why was my word sheet rejected

## Add a language

Pick the language you want to write for, then fill in the boxes. A language you finish can be exported and used on another computer.

Questions: how do I add a language to Trindle; how do I translate Trindle into my language

## If a sheet is rejected

The card says why, in a few words: the file was too big, was not plain text, was not a Trindle word sheet, or had been changed in a place that is not for words. Export a fresh sheet and try again.
