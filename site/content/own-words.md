You can change every word Trindle shows. You can say "Open it, brew" where Trindle says "Click here to open the file", or write a whole language that Trindle doesn't have yet. Your words are saved in one file, called a word sheet. Every Trindle program reads it, so one change shows everywhere.

You'll find Your own words in Settings, under Region and language. It's a card with that name.

## Change a word

1. Open Settings, then Region and language.
2. In Your own words, choose the language the sheet is for.
3. Under Find a word, type part of what Trindle says now. Trindle lists the words that match, each with a box next to Trindle's English.
4. Type your own words in the box. Anything you leave alone stays as it was.

## Share your words

Export saves the sheet as a file you can give to anyone. Import lets you use a sheet somebody gave you. You pass the file on yourself, like any other file. Trindle never sends it anywhere.

Trindle checks an imported sheet as a whole before it uses any of it. The sheet must be a plain text file that Trindle made, with nothing added that isn't words. If anything is wrong, Trindle installs nothing and the card tells you what the problem was.

## Add a language

Choose the language you want to write for, then fill in the boxes. When you've finished a language, you can export it and use it on another computer.

Questions: how do I add a language to Trindle; how do I translate Trindle into my language

## If a sheet is rejected

The card gives the reason in a few words. The file was too big, it wasn't plain text, it wasn't a Trindle word sheet, or it had been changed in a place that isn't meant for words. Export a fresh sheet and try again.
